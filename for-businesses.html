How It Works

1. List Your Business

Contact us to get your business listed. We'll set up your profile with your name, category (restaurant, pharmacy, or delivery), description, and contact details.

2. Set Up Your Menu

Add your products or services with prices, descriptions, and images. Customers will browse your menu and add items to their cart.

3. Receive Orders

When a customer places an order, it's sent directly to WhatsApp Where we'll forward and you'd confirm availability, arrange payment, and arrange delivery.

4. Grow Your Business

As you receive orders and build your reputation, you'll appear higher in search results. More visibility means more customers.

📌 Important: You are responsible for:

  • Confirming order availability
  • Arranging payment with the customer
  • Managing delivery or pickup
  • Customer service and satisfaction
